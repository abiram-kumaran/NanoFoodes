# NanoFoodes
Nano foodes – Helping Small Food Producers Sell Beyond Their Town


A home-based pickle maker may have enough jars to fill her first order from another town and still face three questions. Which batch meets the shop’s shelf-life requirement? What details should she send the buyer? Will she earn anything after delivery?

Nano foodes is a multilingual, Android-first assistant that helps small food producers answer these questions. A producer can speak about a batch in Tamil or English, photograph existing records and paste a retailer’s enquiry. The system connects her recipe, label, batch details, costs and supporting documents. It then identifies which batch she can offer, explains any missing information and calculates what the order would leave her after delivery. She reviews the label, cost and quotation before sharing the offer with the buyer.
